Delete your account

Last updated 5 November 2026

This page covers Frost Week Tracker — the planner at planner.froststudio.org and its Android app — made by the developer of Frost Studio.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open the planner, on the web or in the Android app, and sign in.
  2. Tap your picture at the top right to open your profile.
  3. Optional: choose download my data to keep a copy.
  4. Choose delete account, type delete, and confirm with Google or with your password.

Your account is deleted at once.

Or ask by email

If you can’t sign in any more, email joshoffrost8@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, asking for your account to be deleted. It is deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply when it’s done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address and sign-in details.
  • Every week: tasks, notes, and the focus, reward and affirmation lines.
  • Your standing tasks, groups, long-term goals and profile picture.
  • Calendar events imported from Google Calendar. Access to your Google Calendar is revoked as well.

Nothing is kept afterwards. The data is removed from the live database right away; the hosting providers’ routine backups expire on their own schedule.

Data that was never in an account

If you used the planner without signing in, your data only ever lived on that device. Clear the site’s data in your browser, or uninstall the app, to remove it.
