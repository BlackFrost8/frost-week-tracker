## Store listing

**App name** (30 max): `Frost Week Tracker`

**Short description** (80 max, this is 78):

```
A calm weekly planner. One ring for your week, today's tasks front and centre.
```

**Full description** (4000 max):

```
Frost is a weekly planner built around one idea: you should be able to see your whole week at a glance, and today's work without looking for it.

TODAY, FRONT AND CENTRE
Today gets the card. Add tasks as fast as you can type, tick them off with one tap, and move anything that belongs on another day.

ONE RING FOR THE WEEK
Every task you finish fills the ring. A pace line shows whether you're ahead of or behind an even week, and last week's score sits underneath so you can beat it.

THE WHOLE WEEK IN ONE STRIP
Seven days across the top, each with its own progress. Tap any day to plan it.

PLAN THE WEEK ON PURPOSE
Write a focus for the week, a reward for hitting it, and a line to keep you going.

GROUPS AND GOALS
Sort tasks into groups like School, Health or Work, each with its own icon and progress bar. Let a group show what's coming up in later weeks, so a deadline three weeks out doesn't surprise you. Keep long-term goals beside the week they're for.

YOUR ROUTINE, AUTOMATICALLY
Set standing tasks once (gym on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, reading every weekday) and every new week arrives with them already in place.

GOOGLE CALENDAR, READ-ONLY
Bring in your Google Calendar events so lessons and appointments show on the right day. Frost only reads your calendar; it never changes it.

WORKS WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT, SYNCS WITH ONE
Use it straight away with nothing to sign up for. Sign in with Google or email and your weeks follow you across your phone, laptop and school Chromebook. It works offline and catches up when you're back online.

MAKE IT YOURS
Five themes, or pick your own two colours and the whole app re-colours to match. A focus timer and a privacy screen are built in.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads. No tracking. No analytics. Your data is never sold or shared. Download everything as a file, or delete your account and all of its data from inside the app, at any time.
```

## App content (Policy → App content)

**Privacy policy:** `https://planner.froststudio.org/privacy.html`

**Ads:** No, the app does not contain ads.

**App access:** "All or some functionality is restricted."
Most of the app works signed out. Syncing, the profile and account deletion need
an account, so give reviewers one:
1. Create a test account with **email and password** (not Google) on the live site.
2. In Console, enter its email and password, with this instruction: *"Tap
   'sign in' (top right), then 'use an email and password instead'. Account
   deletion is under the profile picture → 'delete account'."*

**Content rating:** Run the questionnaire with category **Utility, Productivity,
Communication, or Other**. Answers: no violence, sexual content, profanity,
drugs, gambling or user-to-user interaction/sharing (all data is private to the
account), no location sharing, no digital purchases. The expected rating is
**Everyone / PEGI 3**.

**Target audience:** Choose **13 and over** (13–15, 16–17, 18+). If you include
under-13s, the app falls under the Families policy, with stricter requirements
on sign-in, third-party SDKs and data. Google sign-in and Firebase need extra
review there. Don't tick under-13 unless the app is meant for children.

**News app:** No. **COVID-19 tracing:** No. **Government app:** No.
**Financial features:** None. **Health:** None (the app doesn't track health
data; "Health" is just a group name you can type).

## Data safety

**Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** Yes.

**Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?** Yes (HTTPS only).

**Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?** Yes.
- In-app: profile → delete account
- Web: `https://planner.froststudio.org/delete-account.html`

**Shared with third parties:** None. Firebase (Google) stores the data as a
*service provider* acting for the app, which Play does not count as sharing.

**Collected** (all only when the user signs in; signing in is optional, so mark
each as **optional**). Each type is **not processed ephemerally**, and the
purpose is **App functionality** plus **Account management** where noted:

| Play data type | What it is in Frost | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal info → Name | Display name from Google or the account | Account management, App functionality |
| Personal info → Email address | Sign-in email | Account management |
| Personal info → User IDs | Firebase user id | Account management |
| Photos and videos → Photos | Profile picture, if the user uploads one | App functionality |
| Calendar → Calendar events | Imported Google Calendar events (title, date, time) | App functionality |
| App activity → Other user-generated content | Tasks, notes, groups, goals, focus/reward lines | App functionality |

**Not collected:** location, contacts, financial info, health, messages, audio,
files, web history, device or other IDs, crash logs, diagnostics, analytics.

> **Check one thing before submitting:** Firebase Authentication itself
> handles IP addresses and user-agent strings during sign-in. Google's
> "Firebase data disclosure for Google Play" page lists what each Firebase SDK
> collects. Read the Authentication row and add anything it says must be
> declared. That page is the authority on this, not this draft.
