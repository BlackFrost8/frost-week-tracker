What FROST is

FROST is a weekly planner at planner.froststudio.org. You plan tasks for each day, keep notes on days, sort tasks into groups, and track longer-term goals. It can also show events from your Google Calendar on the matching days.

What it stores

If you don’t sign in, everything stays in your browser’s storage on that device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

If you sign in, FROST stores:

  • Your name, email address and profile photo, from Google or from the email you sign up with. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Your weeks: tasks, notes, and the focus, reward and affirmation lines.
  • Your standing tasks, standing notes, groups, goals, and a profile picture if you upload one.
  • Calendar events you import (see below).

These are kept in Google Cloud Firestore in the United States, under your account, and a copy is kept in your browser so the planner works without a connection. Access rules let only your signed-in account read or write your data from the app.

Google Calendar

FROST only touches your calendar if you choose connect google calendar in settings. When you do:

  • It asks Google for read-only access (calendar.readonly). It never creates, changes or deletes anything in your calendar.
  • It reads the list of your calendars, and the events on the ones you have turned on in Google Calendar, from two weeks before the current week to twelve weeks after it.
  • From each event it keeps only the title, date and start time. It reads your own reply to an invitation only to leave out events you declined. It does not keep descriptions, locations, guests or attachments.
  • The imported events are saved to your account and your browser, so they show on the right days, including on your other devices.
  • The access token Google gives FROST is kept only in your browser tab’s session storage. It expires within an hour and is never stored on a server.
  • disconnect deletes the imported events from your account and revokes the token. You can also remove FROST’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

FROST’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What FROST doesn’t do

  • It doesn’t sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • It doesn’t show ads, and has no analytics or tracking.
  • It doesn’t use your data, including calendar data, for anything but showing you your own planner. It is not used to train AI models.
  • Nobody reads your data. The developer has administrative access to the database as its owner, and uses it only to keep the service running or when you ask for help.

Services FROST relies on

  • Firebase (Google) for sign-in and storage.
  • GitHub Pages to host the site.
  • Google Fonts for the typefaces.

These providers may log basic request details, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Removing your data

  • Signing out removes your account’s copy from that device once everything has been saved to your account.
  • Disconnecting Google Calendar deletes the imported events.
  • Data from before you signed in can be cleared by clearing this site’s data in your browser.
  • To delete your account and everything stored with it, email joshoffrost8@gmail.com. It is deleted within 30 days.
